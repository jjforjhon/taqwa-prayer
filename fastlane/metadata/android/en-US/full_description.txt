Taqwa — Salah & Worship Tracker

A beautiful, fully offline Android app for Muslims to track their salah (prayer) and worship based on Salafi Islam (Ahlul Hadith manhaj).

Features:
• Prayer Tracker — Track all 5 daily prayers with Jama'ah support
• Sunnah Mu'akkadah — 12 established Rakat per the Hadith of Umm Habibah (Sahih Muslim 727)
• General Daily Actions — Tahiyatul Masjid, Ishraq, and voluntary prayers
• Adhkar — Morning, Evening, Sleep, and Masnoon Du'as with authentic hadith sources
• Quran Tracker — Log reading sessions, track Juz progress and Khatmah count
• Stats Dashboard — Streak, weekly chart, perfect days, and more
• Offline Prayer Times — Calculated locally using Umm al-Qura method (no internet required)
• Prayer Notifications — Local reminders for each of the 5 daily prayers

Based on:
• The Quran and Sunnah upon the Manhaj of the Salaf
• Umm al-Qura University prayer time calculation (Saudi Arabia)
• Standard Asr method (Shafi'i/Maliki/Hanbali/Ahlul Hadith)
• All adhkar sourced from Sahih al-Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, and other canonical collections
• Permanent Committee for Scholarly Research and Ifta (Saudi Arabia)

Privacy:
• 100% offline — no data leaves your device
• No accounts, no servers, no tracking
• All data stored locally via Android internal storage

Zero proprietary Google Play Services dependencies.
Built with Capacitor (open-source).
